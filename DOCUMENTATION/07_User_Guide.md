# 07 – User Guide

## Citizen
1. Register and sign in.
2. Select Submit a grievance.
3. Choose district/category, enter subject and detailed description.
4. Optionally request AI analysis and review its suggestions.
5. Attach allowed supporting files and submit.
6. Save the generated reference number.
7. Use Track grievance or Dashboard to follow status.
8. After resolution, submit feedback or request reopening.

## CMO administrator
1. Sign in with CMO demo account for local testing.
2. Review all grievances and statistics.
3. Open a grievance, inspect attachments and AI suggestions.
4. Assign the relevant department and update status/remarks.
5. Review department action-taken report and mark the grievance resolved or request further action.
6. Review district/department statistics and contact enquiries.

## Department officer
1. Sign in with the officer credentials provisioned for the relevant department.
2. Review department-assigned grievances.
3. Add investigation notes and action-taken report.
4. Submit report to CMO for review (Under Review).
