# 01 – Project Overview

## Problem
Citizens need a clear way to submit public-service grievances, track progress and receive outcomes. Administrators need a traceable way to route issues to departments and review action taken.

## Objectives
- Provide citizen registration and grievance submission.
- Generate unique grievance references.
- Route grievances from CMO to departments.
- Record every status transition, timestamp and actor.
- Assist triage with a Groq LLM while retaining human decision-making.
- Provide role-specific dashboards and reporting.

## Scope
Public pages, citizen portal, CMO administration, department officer workspace, contact enquiries, attachments, feedback and status tracking.

## Roles
- Citizen: own grievances, tracking, feedback and reopening.
- CMO administrator: review, assign, remark, update status and view statistics.
- Department officer: view grievances assigned to their department and submit action reports for CMO review.
